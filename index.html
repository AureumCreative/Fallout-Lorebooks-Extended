About the Package

The Extended Lorebooks Bring Even More of the World to You! 🌎

  • More than isolated lore entries. These Lorebooks are built as an interconnected web of knowledge: the bot's scenario definition forms the foundation, while related entries reference one another and build upon that foundation.
  • No need to memorize keywords — let the AI connect the dots. This structure allows the AI to naturally introduce characters, technologies, factions, locations, and other Fallout 4 elements into the roleplay — even when you never explicitly mention them yourself.
  • Expand the interconnected knowledge network. The Extended Lorebook Package weaves even more lore into this system, giving the AI additional opportunities to naturally enrich your roleplay and create a Commonwealth that feels deeper, more alive, and faithful to the Fallout 4 experience.

The Package Features

112 Lorebook Entries

112 viewable, fully customizable entries, including 38 entries exclusive to this package.

Flexible Lorebook Setup

Import the scripts in just a few clicks and choose between one All-in-One Lorebook or separate Companions, Worldbuilding, and Factions & Locations Lorebooks.

RPG Bot Essentials

All necessary definition prompts and 10 intro messages for creating your own Fallout RPG bot.

Project Compatibility

Designed to work alongside my Deacon Bot Package and future Fallout 4 projects.

Beginner-Friendly Guides

Step-by-step guides for creating your own Fallout RPG bot and setting up the Lorebooks.

Optional Images

5 AI-generated and post-processed images are included, though you can use them or any other images you prefer.

JLLM Compatible

The package is designed to work with JLLM, although a quality proxy is generally recommended for the best roleplay experience.
